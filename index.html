      How to play

      You skipped CSE 2004 and missed today's quiz code. It's always one general-knowledge word, the same for everyone today. Type guesses into the quiz page on your laptop before class ends. Your friends in the back row will help, but only so much.

      • Your friends won't text you the code.

        The syllabus calls sharing access codes academic dishonesty, so they send hints instead. The first one comes free.

      • Each hint costs 7 minutes.

        Your friends are in class, and replies take time. Late work loses points: 1.25% for every minute after 1:05.

      • A wrong code costs 1 minute.

        Every attempt takes a moment, so typing every word you know doesn't pay. A near miss is free: you'll be told to check the spelling.

      • The quiz closes at 2:20.

        That's when class ends. After that it's 0 / 2, and you'll see everyone Thursday.

      • Your lowest three days are dropped.

        Just like the syllabus drops your three lowest quiz grades, so one bad morning won't sink you.
